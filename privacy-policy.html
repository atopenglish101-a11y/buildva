Buildva Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Buildva – Picking Numbers ("Buildva", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Buildva mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

Buildva is designed to require as little personal information as possible.

Depending on how you use the game, Buildva may process:

  • Player names or names entered when joining a game.
  • Game codes used to create or join multiplayer games.
  • Game information such as player status, scores, selected numbers and game progress.
  • Technical information necessary for the application and its online services to operate.
  • Information provided if you contact us for support.

Buildva does not require you to provide information such as your home address, telephone number, or payment information in order to play the game.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Buildva may be used to:

  • Create and operate multiplayer games.
  • Allow players to join games using game codes.
  • Display player names, scores and game progress to participants.
  • Maintain and synchronize online game sessions.
  • Provide and improve the Buildva service.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Detect and prevent misuse of the service.

3. Firebase

Buildva uses Firebase services provided by Google to support certain online functionality, including multiplayer game data and application services.

Information processed through Firebase is handled according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Voice Communication

Buildva may provide an optional voice communication feature for players during a game.

If voice communication is available and you choose to use it, audio is transmitted between players using the communication technology provided by the application.

Voice communication is not required to play Buildva.

5. Advertising

Buildva may display advertisements in the future.

If advertising is introduced, third-party advertising providers may process certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

This section will be updated when advertising services are introduced.

6. Data Retention

Game information may be stored for the period necessary to provide multiplayer functionality and maintain the service.

We may remove game information when it is no longer needed.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by Buildva. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

8. Children's Privacy

Buildva is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Buildva, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Third-Party Services

Buildva may use third-party services to provide functionality, hosting, analytics, communications, advertising or other technical services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Your Choices

You can choose whether or not to use optional features such as voice communication.

You can also stop using Buildva at any time.

If you have questions about information associated with your use of Buildva, you may contact us.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published with a new "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Buildva, please contact us through the contact information provided on the official Buildva website.
